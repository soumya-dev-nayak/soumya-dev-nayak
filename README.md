## About

I'm **Soumya Dev Nayak**, a final-year B.Tech ECE student working across **VLSI, analog and digital IC design, RTL development, hardware implementation, and verification** — with embedded systems and IoT as a hands-on complement to the core hardware work.

My work ranges from designing and simulating **analog circuits at the transistor level** to developing **digital systems in Verilog**, building **processor architectures**, performing **hardware verification**, implementing designs on **FPGAs**, and exploring complete **RTL-to-GDSII ASIC flows** using open-source EDA tools.

I enjoy understanding systems at the level where theory becomes hardware — designing, simulating, implementing, verifying, debugging, and iterating on real circuits and architectures.
